Privacy policy

A plain account of what we collect, why we need it, and the choices you have.

Effective 4 October 2026

Who we are

Human Works is the services arm of Human School, based in India. For questions about this policy or your information, email hello@humanschool.in.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us when you contact us, discuss a project, or engage us for work. This may include your name, work email, phone number, company, role, project details, and materials you share with us. Our website may also receive basic technical records such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and request time through our hosting and security providers.

How we use it

  • Respond to enquiries and prepare proposals.
  • Deliver the work we agree to do.
  • Operate, secure, and improve the website and our services.
  • Meet legal, accounting, and contractual duties.

We do not sell personal information.

Client and project data

Projects can involve real business information. The scope, permitted systems, confidentiality, retention, and ownership of deliverables are set in the client agreement. Please do not share personal, confidential, regulated, or production data with us unless the engagement expressly allows it.

Who receives information

We share information only as needed with providers that help us run the service, such as website hosting, email, document, and scheduling providers, or when required by law. Some providers may process data outside India under their own safeguards.

Cookies and local storage

This site does not use advertising cookies. If we add optional analytics or marketing technology, we will update this notice and ask for consent where required.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, our agreements, and legal obligations. We use reasonable access controls and service-provider safeguards, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you have provided, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or raise a grievance. Email hello@humanschool.in. We may need to verify your identity and may retain records where law or a contract requires it.

Updates

We may revise this policy when our services or legal duties change. The effective date above shows the latest version.
